## 1. The power fantasy

**Power fantasy** is the test for every decision. The player is the most powerful *and* the kindest being on Earth. Within 10 seconds of gaining control they are threading between skyscrapers at speed with the cape cracking behind them; within 60 seconds they have used three different powers.

Tone: hopeful, bright, colourful comic-book realism. Daylight-first palette (cobalt blue, crimson, gold against clean sky). Superman rescues first and smashes second; he never kills. Careless destruction has visible, soft consequences (section 11).

The three classic promises are the design pillars. Each gets a signature mechanic polished to a shine:

1. **Faster than a speeding bullet** — the speed-tier flight model (section 7).
2. **More powerful than a locomotive** — weighty strength, throws, catches, and train-stopping (section 8).
3. **Able to leap tall buildings in a single bound** — the charged super-leap (section 7).
