## 11. Incidents: the rescue loop

Metropolis needs people to save. An **Incident Director** keeps 3 – 6 incidents alive within 3 km of the player, weighted by district, severity (1 – 3) and time of day. Incidents are found by sight, the news ticker, compass markers, and **super hearing** (section 8.6).

### 11.1 Incident catalogue (build at least these 14; each has several valid power solutions)

1. **Runaway train** on the Elevated or the Metro-Narrows rail deck: stop it (strength).
2. **Bridge span failure** on Centennial or Seagate: catch and hold the span, carry people off.
3. **Skyscraper fire** with trapped people: X-ray to find them, freeze breath and super breath to fight the fire, carry them out.
4. **Plane in distress** over the bay: catch it and guide it to a safe landing at the harbour or airfield.
5. **Bank heist** at Metropolis Bank with hostages: disarm with precision heat vision, catch bullets in focus time, shield hostages. The vault is lead-lined.
6. **Runaway school bus** near the Elementary School: catch and stop it.
7. **Factory explosion** at Stagg Ind. or Steelworks with a molten-metal spill: freeze breath and strength.
8. **Prison break** at Stryker Penitentiary: restrain escapees non-lethally and hold the perimeter.
9. **Ship adrift** in Metropolis Harbor, with an oil fire: push the ship, freeze the flames.
10. **Collapsing crane or scaffold** in Southside: catch and set down.
11. **Tidal surge** in Hob's Bay: raise an ice wall with freeze breath or push the wave back with super breath.
12. **Multi-car pile-up** on an avenue or in a tunnel: lift and clear vehicles.
13. **Floodlight tower collapse** at Shuster Arena: catch and hold.
14. **Gas leak** in a tower: X-ray to find the source, super breath to ventilate.

Stretch incidents: a meteor on re-entry (intercept at altitude), a LexCorp R&D kryptonite accident (survive with reduced powers), and a **rogue-drone assault** on Southside that escalates to a giant armoured mech set piece using every power, offered after a set number of rescues as the finale of a "Metropolis day".

### 11.2 Rules of play

- **Non-lethal.** Enemies are criminals and drones; they are disarmed, restrained, knocked out or shut down. No gore.
- **Hope and collateral.** *Hope* rises with saves, kindness (waving back, posing for photos) and clean solutions. *Collateral* rises with careless destruction (sonic booms over dense streets, wrecked vehicles, damaged buildings). High collateral changes NPC reactions (fewer cheers, a few "Careful!" barks) and the tone of the newspaper headlines. It never causes a fail state.
- **Results card** after each incident: people saved, damage, time, and a one-line Daily Planet headline.
- **No required story.** The game is a sandbox with an optional daily arc; the player can simply fly and play.
