## 2. Priorities and effort budget

Spend effort in this proportion. When two tasks compete, the higher row wins.

| # | Area | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flight and movement feel (section 7) | 30% |
| 2 | Powers and their juice (section 8) | 20% |
| 3 | Visual quality: hero, cape, Play sequence (5.2), lighting, sky, VFX, post (sections 6, 12) | 20% |
| 4 | Metropolis scale, landmarks, streaming (section 9) | 15% |
| 5 | Living world: NPCs, traffic, incidents (sections 10, 11) | 8% |
| 6 | Audio (section 12) | 4% |
| 7 | Main menu and UI | 3% |

The main menu is a one-sitting job (section 5.1). Finish it, verify it once, and return to rows 1–4. The Play sequence (5.2) is *not* capped: it is the player's first impression of the hero and belongs to row 3.
